Our story

Fifty-five years of the same promise.

Dr. Gianfranco "Johnny" Spellman opened this clinic in 1971 with a simple idea: small-animal care that treats every pet like the family's own. The neighborhood has changed a lot since then — the way we handle your dog or cat hasn't.

Today we're still focused exclusively on dogs and cats: wellness exams, vaccinations, spay & neuter, surgery, dental care and boarding — with time taken to explain everything before it happens.

Independently owned. While many Austin clinics have been bought by corporate groups, North Lamar has stayed family-run — decisions made by veterinarians, not investors.
